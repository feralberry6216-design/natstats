Society & living: how Americans are doing

Income, poverty and health coverage move slowly — but they move. Switch between 2023 and 2025 below to see what changed in two years: median income rose by nearly $7,000, and the poverty rate fell.

Life expectancy fell during the pandemic, then rebounded: 78.4 years in 2023 and 78.9 in 2024, above the pre-pandemic level of 78.8 in 2019.

Reading the numbers

Median household income is the income of the household in the middle — half of households earn more, half earn less. The poverty rate counts people whose income falls below the federal poverty threshold. Both come from the Census Bureau's annual income and poverty release, usually published each September.
